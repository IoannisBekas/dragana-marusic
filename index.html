ChapterIV / VI
IV

Letter

Read at your own pace
Beograd · IV · 2026

There is a kind of attention that begins as patience, then becomes something else — closer to listening than looking. The studio learned this from the long hours, from the silence after a roll of film ends. The camera is not the subject of the work. The subject is the room, the breath, the slow agreement between two people that something true is allowed to happen.

We rarely speak during a session. Words narrow the field of what is possible. Instead we move, we wait, we let the light decide. When the picture finds itself, it is rarely the one we expected.

What follows in this volume is a small selection from eight years. We have removed our favourites; the favourites are too easy. What remains is harder, slower, and — we hope — more honest.
